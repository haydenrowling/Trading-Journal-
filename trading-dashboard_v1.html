05 — Mental Checklist  Click each question to confirm — be honest
Is this trade more likely to head to my TP than my SL, based purely on structure?
01 · Probability & edge
Am I taking this trade for any reason other than it following my plan completely — no exceptions, no bending the rules?
02 · Emotional honesty — a "yes" here means DO NOT take the trade
Would I take this exact trade after a loss? After a win? The answer should be yes — if not, I'm trading emotionally.
03 · Emotional neutrality
Is my risk fully defined and accepted? My SL placement is objective, my lot size is pre-calculated, and losing 1R right now is completely fine.
04 · Risk acceptance
Would I still be comfortable if this trade stops out immediately after entry? If not, my SL is too tight or my risk is too high.
05 · Stop-out comfort
Have I waited for full entry model confirmation — or am I anticipating / trying to catch a better price? Patience is the edge.
06 · Patience & confirmation
Is there a high-impact news event in the next 2 hours that I have not already accounted for? If yes, I either reduce risk or do not enter.
07 · News & macro awareness
Can I walk away after placing this trade and let it play out without interference? If I feel the urge to manage it immediately, I'm not ready.
08 · Post-entry discipline
Am I in the right mental state to trade? Not tired, frustrated, euphoric, or distracted. Trading requires a clear head — full stop.
09 · Mental & emotional state
Have I identified where the trade is wrong — and do I accept that level as the real invalidation, not just a price I don't want to see?
